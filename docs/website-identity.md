# PRD — Aeternum Talent Solutions Website

## Product Overview

**Product Name:** Aeternum Talent Solutions Website  
**Tagline:** _Where Talent Meets Excellence._

The website will present Aeternum Talent Solutions as a remote staffing and business growth partner for online businesses. Its purpose is to clearly explain the company’s value, showcase services, build trust, and convert visitors into leads.

---

## Objective

The website should:

- Introduce Aeternum Talent Solutions clearly
- Show what services are offered
- Explain why the company is different
- Build trust with businesses looking for remote teams
- Encourage visitors to book a call or send an inquiry

---

## Target Audience

### Primary Audience

- Online business owners
- Ecommerce brands
- Coaches and agencies
- Founders of growing companies
- Businesses needing customer support, sales, or operations help

### Secondary Audience

- Companies exploring offshore staffing
- Businesses frustrated by high turnover or low-performing remote hires

---

## Main Goals

- Generate qualified leads
- Present services in a clear and strong way
- Position Aeternum as a strategic partner, not just a staffing agency
- Highlight low attrition, pre-trained talent, and performance-ready teams

---

## Core Message

Aeternum Talent Solutions helps growing online businesses build stable, high-performing remote teams with pre-trained Filipino professionals who are ready to contribute from day one.
